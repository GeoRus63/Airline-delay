Predictive performance and trade-off

On the prespecified August UTC prediction-window test (n = 560,077), the frozen random forest achieved pooled MAE/RMSE of 13.655/42.917 minutes, outperforming all three baselines on both aggregate metrics. Relative to the training-median baseline, MAE decreased by 4.345 minutes (24.14%; paired seven-day moving-block 95% interval: −6.647 to −1.985). This aggregate gain involved an outcome-defined trade-off: for ordinary cases (target <60 minutes), MAE increased from 6.745 to 8.279, although RMSE decreased from 14.641 to 10.816; for severe cases (target ≥60 minutes), MAE decreased from 134.545 to 69.325. Summed absolute error increased by 0.784 million minutes in ordinary cases and decreased by 3.217 million minutes in severe cases. Severe cases represented 8.81% of observations but accounted for 44.71% of RF absolute error and 94.21% of squared error. These retrospective strata describe error concentration and cannot define an operational model-switching rule.

RQ1 scope and support

RQ1 describes April–August, including the development months, and is not an independent August confirmation. Under the frozen reference standardization, 24 of the 25 supported airports had negative point estimates. FLL was the only positive estimate (+0.436 minutes), but its interval included zero (−2.403 to 3.012). The 278,633 retained connections represented 57.81% of the full raw main sample and 92.57% of the raw sample within the selected airports. Only 15 airports met the monthly support requirements in August; the remaining ten stayed unavailable. Relative to the ≥15-minute threshold, sign agreement was 20/25 for >0 and 24/25 for ≥5, despite high rank correlations. These descriptive associations do not establish airport-level causal effects or multiplicity-adjusted rankings.

Uncertainty and verification status

The six date-block intervals are individual comparisons conditional on the frozen models, the 31 UTC prediction dates and the specified resampling scheme; they do not provide simultaneous coverage or establish performance in other periods. Independent metric recomputation and archival audits passed. Full feature reconstruction and refitting in R30 remain in progress, so full numerical reproducibility has not yet been established. Because development retained July predictions with August-maturing outcomes, the study remains retrospective and does not simulate a selection process completed by 1 August.
